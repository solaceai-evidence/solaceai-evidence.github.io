A risk assessment tool for AI-generated evidence

Can you rely on this AI-generated evidence report?

DEEPER walks you through six things worth checking before an AI-generated evidence report informs a real decision. It takes around 45 minutes, and you don't need to be a methods expert.

At the end you get a plain-language summary: what looks sound, what raised a flag, what nobody could reasonably check — and what you can do about each.

It's a risk assessment, not a verdict

DEEPER tells you where the risk sits and how large it looks. It can't tell you whether the report's answer is true.

Some things nobody can check

Two items are hard to assess for anyone outside the topic. We flag those, offer a lighter check, and record what stayed unknown.

You leave with a narrative, not a number

The summary sets out what was found, what it means for the report, and what you might do next — in words you can hand to someone else.

Reference

The DEEPER framework

Six domains, 25 items, one rating scale. Every item is answered Yes, Probably yes, Probably no, No, or No information — the same scale used in risk-of-bias tools, so a hedged answer is a legitimate answer.

Assessment runs in one stage with stop-first logic: if a report fails a critical item, you can stop there.

How a domain gets its rating

Low risk — every item in the domain answered yes or probably yes.

High risk — any item answered no or probably no.

Stop conditions — data that doesn't correspond to the cited sources, or references that don't exist. Neither can be mitigated.
